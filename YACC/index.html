YACC

A FEUP student team building a 1:10 scale autonomous car for the Bosch Future Mobility Challenge.

Students, a car and a miniature city

YACC brings together students who develop, test and tune a 1:10 scale autonomous vehicle. The car has to drive through a miniature city on its own, following the signs and reacting to whatever it meets on the way.

The project is mentored by two lecturers from the Department of Informatics Engineering (DEI) at the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP). It is built to last: the team wants to compete every year and hand what it learns to the people who come next.

BFMC 2026: our first time

6th place in the final Best newcomer team

We took part in the Bosch Future Mobility Challenge for the first time in 2026. We reached the final, finished 6th and were the best team among the newcomers.

BFMC is an international competition run by the Bosch Engineering Center Cluj, in Romania. University teams develop autonomous driving and connectivity algorithms for 1:10 scale vehicles that drive in a miniature city.
